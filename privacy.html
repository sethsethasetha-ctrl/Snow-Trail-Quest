Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PHWKKWHP (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Snow Trail Quest — drawride (the “App”).

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The App does not require an account, does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, camera, or health data.

Game progress and device storage

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. This information remains on the device and is not transmitted to us. Removing the App may remove locally stored progress.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

The App does not use cross-app tracking, advertising, analytics, or third-party data brokers. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate third-party services that receive personal information. Apple may process information as part of the App Store and operating system according to Apple’s own policies.

Children’s privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information, it is not designed to collect information from children or adults.

Security

Local game data is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. No method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information shown on the App Store listing.
